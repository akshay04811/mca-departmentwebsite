Message from the Head of the Department

"Welcome to the Department of Computer Applications at Mohandas College of Engineering & Technology (MCET).

In an era defined by rapid technological disruptions—from generative artificial intelligence and cloud computing to big data architectures and intelligent automation—our primary mission is to cultivate versatile software engineers, problem solvers, and innovators. The MCA programme at MCET is designed to impart not just technical knowledge, but the critical thinking and adaptability required to succeed in the global IT ecosystem.

Our curriculum blends rigorous academic foundations with intensive laboratory sessions, project-based learning, and continuous industry engagement. We encourage our students to look beyond textbook definitions, actively contribute to open-source software, participate in hackathons, and uphold the highest standards of professional ethics.

I warmly invite aspiring students to explore the myriad opportunities in our department and embark on a rewarding journey towards technological excellence."

— Dr. K. Radhakrishnan Nair
Professor & Head, Department of Computer Applications
