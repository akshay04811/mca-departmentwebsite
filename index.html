About the Department

The Department of Computer Applications at Mohandas College of Engineering & Technology was established to offer high-quality postgraduate education in computer applications and information technology.

We offer a comprehensive 2-year Master of Computer Applications (MCA) programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). Our focus is on experiential learning, rigorous algorithmic problem solving, software engineering methodologies, and real-world industrial projects.

With state-of-the-art computer labs, high-speed campus networks, dedicated faculty, and active industry collaborations, we prepare our graduates for rewarding careers in leading global IT enterprises.

📋 Programme Overview & Eligibility

Degree: Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters) Full-Time Regular Programme

Affiliation: APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Approved by AICTE

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Passed BCA / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent degree.
  • Passed B.Sc. / B.Com. / B.A. with Mathematics at 10+2 Level or at Graduation Level.
  • Obtained at least 50% marks (45% for candidates belonging to reserved category) in the qualifying examination.
  • Valid score in Kerala MCA Entrance Examination conducted by CEE Kerala.

💡 Academic Focus & Career Scope

Core Focus Areas:

  • Advanced Data Structures, Object-Oriented Software Design, and Cloud Computing.
  • Full-Stack Web Development, Mobile Application Engineering, and DevOps.
  • Database Administration, Big Data Systems, and Information Security.
  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Analytics.

Career Opportunities:

  • Software Development Engineer (SDE) & Full-Stack Developer
  • Cloud Solution Architect & Systems Engineer
  • Database Administrator & Data Analyst
  • QA Automation Engineer & Cybersecurity Specialist
